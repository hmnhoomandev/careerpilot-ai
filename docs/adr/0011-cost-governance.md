# ADR-0011: CHF 0 Development Budget

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-09

## Decision

Local services, containers, fakes, emulators, and safe free tooling are defaults.
No paid resource, billing enablement, paid call, or recurring service starts
without explicit approval. Free-tier use requires quota and conversion-risk
monitoring.

## Consequences

If payment becomes necessary, work stops before cost and presents the provider,
service, exact need, one-time/monthly estimate, and free/local alternative.
